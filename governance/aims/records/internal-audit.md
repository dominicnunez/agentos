# AIMS internal audit program

Status: **DRAFT — no audit has been performed by this document**

## Objective and scope

The internal audit program will determine whether the approved Agent OS project
AIMS conforms to the organization's own requirements and the applicable
requirements selected from an authorized copy of ISO/IEC 42001, and whether it
is effectively implemented and maintained. Runtime governance inspection, CI,
code review, penetration testing, and evidence exports are audit inputs; none
is the internal audit by itself.

The proposed annual program covers the complete approved AIMS scope, with more
frequent or deeper sampling for material change, Critical or High risk,
incidents, prior findings, supplier change, evidence weakness, or ineffective
controls. Before any certification assessment, the complete scope and required
management-system cycle must be audited.

## Audit plan and independence

Each audit records objectives, criteria, scope, methods, sample rationale,
schedule, auditor, competence, independence and conflicts, participants,
evidence access, and reporting audience. An auditor does not audit work for
which that auditor was operationally responsible unless the accountable
executive records a proportionate compensating independence measure.

Audit criteria use approved project records, applicable obligations, and an
authorized standard copy. Public summaries must not reproduce protected
standard text or disclose confidential evidence.

## Evidence and findings

Evidence must be sufficient, relevant, traceable, integrity-checked where
applicable, and sampled across both design and actual operation. The auditor
records evidence references, observation time, system/version, population and
sample, limitations, and the reasoning connecting evidence to the conclusion.

Findings are classified `Critical`, `High`, `Medium`, or `Low` consistently
with the project risk method and identify the exact criterion, observed
condition, evidence, consequence, owner, correction or corrective-action need,
and due condition. Absence of sampled failure is not proof of universal
effectiveness.

Management owns corrections and corrective actions but cannot rewrite the
auditor's finding. Closure requires evidence that the action was implemented
and an independent effectiveness review. Open material findings are inputs to
risk decisions, release gates, management review, and certification readiness.

## Proposed first audit

The first full internal audit should occur after the scope, policy, roles,
objectives, risk/impact method, operating procedures, and controlled documents
are approved and enough operating evidence exists to test implementation. It
must finish, and material findings must be treated and effectively closed,
before an independent certification assessment is requested.
