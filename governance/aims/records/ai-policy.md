# Artificial intelligence policy

Status: **DRAFT — no approval authority**

## Purpose

Agent OS is developed to help people operate persistent artificial
organizations with durable work, explicit authority, reviewable evidence, and
accountable user control. The project will develop, release, maintain, and
support Agent OS in a manner proportionate to its AI, security, privacy,
reliability, transparency, and affected-party risks.

## Policy commitments

The project proposes to:

1. preserve user authority over financial, physical-world, public or external,
   destructive or irreversible, sensitive-data-boundary, privilege or trust,
   legal or binding, deployment, and trusted-core or security consequences;
2. treat model output, tool output, external Agent content, retrieved context,
   and organizational knowledge as untrusted until admitted through the
   applicable deterministic contract;
3. fail closed when identity, authority, approval, evidence, completion,
   tenant, integrity, or policy state is absent, ambiguous, expired, malformed,
   or inconsistent;
4. maintain separation between authenticated identity, scoped access,
   capability, approval, effect permission, and completion authority;
5. minimize data collection and disclosure, preserve tenant isolation, keep
   secrets outside source control, and require explicit review before a
   sensitive-data boundary expands;
6. record consequential organizational state through closed, versioned Event
   Contracts in the authoritative ledger and preserve deterministic evidence
   for investigation and review;
7. use adaptive model inference only where it is justified and keep policy,
   authority, admission, and completion decisions deterministic;
8. identify foreseeable misuse and affected-party impacts across the product
   lifecycle, implement proportionate treatment, and refuse release or
   deployment when an applicable acceptance gate is not satisfied;
9. provide accurate documentation about capabilities, limitations, known
   risks, and evidence boundaries without claiming that software alone makes
   an operator compliant or that Agent OS is certified; and
10. monitor objectives, incidents, nonconformities, supplier risks, audit
    findings, and control effectiveness, then make evidence-backed corrective
    and continual-improvement decisions.

## Application

This policy applies to work inside the approved project AIMS scope. Everyone
performing in-scope work, including automated systems, must operate within the
approved controls. Automated systems may propose changes and collect evidence;
they may not approve this policy, accept residual risk, determine control
applicability, close audit findings, perform management review, or claim
conformity or certification.

Suspected safety, security, privacy, legal, integrity, or governance incidents
must be contained and escalated through the approved incident process. Policy
exceptions require an explicit, time-bounded, recorded decision by the
accountable authority and cannot override applicable law or contractual duties.

## Review

The proposed review cadence is at least annually and after a material change,
serious incident, significant new legal or contractual obligation, or change
to the AIMS scope. Approval, communication, operating evidence, and review
results remain outstanding while this document is a draft.
