# AIMS management review

Status: **DRAFT**

The accountable executive conducts management review at least annually, before
seeking certification assessment, and after a material incident or change when
ordinary review timing would be inadequate. The review evaluates continuing
suitability, adequacy, effectiveness, and alignment of the AIMS; it is not a
rubber-stamp or a conformity certificate.

## Required inputs

- status and effectiveness of actions from prior management reviews;
- changes in internal and external issues, interested parties, applicable
  obligations, intended purpose, scope, suppliers, technology, data, and
  deployment context;
- AI policy suitability and objective results, trends, misses, and measurement
  limitations;
- AI risk and impact assessments, treatment progress, residual decisions,
  emerging risks, affected-party information, and opportunities;
- incidents, nonconformities, corrective actions, vulnerabilities, complaints,
  contestability or remedy cases, and lessons learned;
- monitoring, model and control evaluation, governance inspection, security,
  privacy, reliability, recovery, and continuity evidence;
- internal and external audit results, open findings, independence, and
  evidence limitations;
- supplier and provider performance, changes, incidents, concentration,
  contracts, data terms, and exit readiness;
- resources, competence, awareness, communication, role conflicts, and
  workload; and
- opportunities for improvement and whether the AIMS still supports project
  strategy and responsible product outcomes.

## Required outputs

The controlled review record identifies the meeting or decision period,
participants and roles, evidence set and versions, conflicts, conclusions, and
explicit decisions about:

- changes to scope, policy, objectives, risks, controls, processes, intended
  use, or documented information;
- accepted, rejected, or escalated residual risks within delegated authority;
- resource, competence, supplier, monitoring, audit, incident, and improvement
  actions with owners and due conditions;
- communication and stakeholder actions;
- release, operation, suspension, retirement, or certification-readiness gates;
  and
- the next review date and follow-up method.

Unanswered material decisions remain open. Models and Agents may assemble
bounded evidence and draft minutes but cannot act as management, accept risk,
approve their own controls, close findings, or determine conformity or
certification.

## Readiness condition

The first review should occur only after approved management-system records and
sufficient operating evidence exist. A meeting held over draft documents with
no operated evidence would not establish effective implementation. Before a
certification assessment, actions affecting readiness must be completed and
their effectiveness verified or transparently presented as open.
