# Control-applicability outcome

Status: **DRAFT**

## Purpose

This record prepares the outcome of the Agent OS project control-applicability
review. It does not reproduce the ISO/IEC 42001 control catalogue and is not a
Statement of Applicability while it remains a draft.

## Current result

`INCOMPLETE`

The result remains incomplete until the approved AIMS scope and obligations are
reviewed against an authorized copy of the standard and every control in that
review basis has an explicit inclusion or exclusion decision and rationale.
Included controls also require an owner, implementation state, evidence,
effectiveness result, exception treatment, and review date.

## Required result evidence

The governed result must identify the authorized-standard review basis, exact
approved register, reviewers and roles, conflicts, incomplete items, and
conclusion. A complete result must be converted to the closed structured
outcome contract, bind the total reviewed, included, excluded, and
rationale-missing counts, prove that every reviewed control was decided with a
rationale, and be approved in the controlled manifest.
